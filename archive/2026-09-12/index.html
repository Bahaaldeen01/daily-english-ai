take it with a grain of salt
/teɪk ɪt wɪð ə greɪn ʌv sɔlt/
خذها ببعض التحفظ
📖 الشرح
يستخدم هذا التعبير للإشارة إلى أنه يجب عدم أخذ شيء ما على محمل الجد بالكامل، أو أن هناك حاجة لتوخي الحذر عند تصديق شيء ما.
💬 أمثلة على الاستخدام
When my friend told me the story, I took it with a grain of salt because he often exaggerates.
You should take the rumors about the new policy with a grain of salt until they are confirmed.
Although the article was interesting, I took it with a grain of salt because it was from an unknown source.
إعلان
📝 مفردات الدرس
rumor
شائعة
"There's a rumor that a new restaurant is opening nearby."
exaggerate
يُبالغ
"Don't exaggerate the story; it's not that dramatic."
confirm
يؤكد
"Can you confirm if the meeting is still on?"
