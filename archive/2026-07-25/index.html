the best of both worlds
/ðə bɛst ʌv boʊθ wɜrldz/
أفضل ما في العالمين
📖 الشرح
تعني وجود أفضل ميزات أو جوانب من حالتين مختلفتين في نفس الوقت. تُستخدم عندما يستفيد الشخص من حالتين إيجابيتين.
💬 أمثلة على الاستخدام
By working from home, I get the best of both worlds: I can spend more time with my family and still finish my projects.
Studying abroad allows students to experience a new culture while receiving a quality education, giving them the best of both worlds.
She loves her job because it offers great benefits and flexibility, so she feels like she has the best of both worlds.
إعلان
