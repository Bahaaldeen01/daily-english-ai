the best of both worlds
/ðə bɛst əv boʊθ wɜrldz/
أفضل ما في العالمين
📖 الشرح
هذه العبارة تُستخدم عندما تكون لديك مزايا من خيارين مختلفين، أي أنك تستفيد من كليهما.
💬 أمثلة على الاستخدام
By working from home, I get the best of both worlds: I save time on commuting and still attend meetings.
She loves her job because it allows her to travel and spend time with her family, giving her the best of both worlds.
Living in the city but having a cabin in the countryside means I have the best of both worlds.
إعلان
